SCAM TAXONOMY

Common Employment Scam Schemes

Predatory techniques recognized and flagged by our heuristic analyzer

Advance Check Cashing / Equipment Fee

Modus Operandi: The fake employer sends a counterfeit cashier's check ($2,000 - $4,000) for "home office equipment" and directs the victim to wire funds to an "approved software vendor".

Consequence: The check bounces days later, leaving the victim legally and financially liable for the entire withdrawn balance.

Telegram / WhatsApp Text-Only Interviews

Modus Operandi: Candidates receive unsolicited messages instructing them to contact an "@hr_recruiter" username on Telegram or WhatsApp for an immediate text interview.

Consequence: Zero video or phone contact occurs. An immediate fake employment offer is issued to harvest SSNs and banking details.

Reshipping / Package Forwarding Fraud

Modus Operandi: Advertised as a "Remote Package Inspector" or "Quality Assistant". The worker receives luxury goods at home and reships them internationally.

Consequence: Merchandise was purchased with stolen credit cards. The worker acts as an unwitting accessory to postal theft.

Money Mule & Crypto Laundering

Modus Operandi: Advertised as a "Payment Processing Agent". The candidate receives money into their personal account and transfers it via Western Union, Zelle, or Bitcoin ATMs.

Consequence: The worker is laundering funds derived from cyber fraud, exposing them to criminal prosecution.

5 Golden Safety Rules for Job Seekers

RULE 01
Never Send Money to Work
Legitimate employers never demand onboarding fees, software licensing charges, or equipment purchases.
RULE 02
Verify Recruiter Email Domains
Corporate recruiters contact candidates from enterprise domains (e.g. name@company.com), not free webmail (@gmail.com).
RULE 03
Demand Synchronous Interviews
If an organization hires you exclusively via text messaging on Telegram without video or phone contact, it is deceptive.
