System Architecture & Fraud Taxonomy

Understand how machine learning detects fraudulent employment listings, and learn to spot predatory schemes before falling victim.

How the AI Detection Pipeline Works

1. NLP & TF-IDF Extraction

The raw text is tokenized, stripped of noise, and transformed into high-dimensional n-gram feature vectors (unigrams and bigrams) with sublinear term-frequency scaling.

2. Supervised Ensemble Classifiers

A Random Forest Classifier and Logistic Regression ensemble evaluate the probabilistic likelihood of fraud based on textual patterns combined with metadata signals (logo presence, screening questions, company profiles).

3. Explainable AI Heuristics

A deterministic rule-augmentation engine identifies specific predatory tactics (e.g., Telegram recruiter tags, cashier's checks, crypto wires) and highlights exact phrases for user explainability.

Common Job Scam Schemes

The Advance Fee / Check Cashing Scam

Mechanism: The 'employer' sends the victim a cashier's check or digital check for $2,000 to $4,000 to buy home-office equipment or software licenses from their 'approved vendor'.

The Catch: The vendor is the scammer. After the victim deposits the check and wires money to the vendor, the original check bounces days later. The victim is held legally and financially responsible by their bank.

Reshipping / Package Forwarding Scam

Mechanism: Advertised as a 'Package Inspector' or 'Shipping Assistant'. The candidate receives luxury items, electronics, or designer clothing at their home and forwards them overseas.

The Catch: The packages were purchased with stolen credit cards. The victim acts as an unwitting accomplice to postal fraud and receives zero payment.

The Telegram / WhatsApp Text Interview Scam

Mechanism: Candidates are contacted via SMS or email claiming their resume was approved and directed to text a recruiter on Telegram, WhatsApp, or Google Chat.

The Catch: No video or voice interview ever occurs. After a few questionnaire questions, an immediate fake offer letter is sent to harvest social security numbers and banking details.

The Money Mule / Crypto Processing Scam

Mechanism: The role is titled 'Payment Processing Agent'. The worker receives deposits into their personal bank account and transfers funds via Western Union, MoneyGram, or Bitcoin ATM.

The Catch: The worker is laundering money from ransomware attacks or identity theft victims, subjecting themselves to severe federal criminal charges.

5 Golden Rules for Job Seekers

1. Never Send Money to Work

Legitimate employers pay you; you never pay them for onboarding, training, background checks, or equipment.

2. Check Domain Email Addresses

Corporate recruiters email from corporate domains (e.g., @microsoft.com), not free webmail (@gmail.com or @yahoo.com).

3. Demand a Real Interview

If the company hires you exclusively over text chat without a video or phone conference, it is almost certainly fraudulent.
